ENCLAVE STATUS GUIDE

How to read the enclave status cards

The cards show whether each IT 359 VSOC enclave is affected and where it is in the response or recovery process. The same six values should be used consistently across incidents.

Not AffectedNo known impact has been identified.
MonitoringNo impact is confirmed, but the enclave is being watched because of a related event.
DegradedFunctionality or confidence is reduced.
ContainedKnown spread has been stopped or limited.
RecoveringTrusted restoration and validation are underway.
RestoredFunctionality and security validation are complete.
